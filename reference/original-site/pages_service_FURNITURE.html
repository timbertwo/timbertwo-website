    Furniture.

    Crafting Stories, Shaping Spaces.

    Adding one or more pieces of Timber Two custom made furniture from sustainable timber means that each piece is unique. Each piece has its own story, and each piece will last a lifetime.

    We make dining tables and bench seats. Bedside tables and bed heads. Hall tables. Mirrors. Entertainment units. Bookcases. Desks. Outdoor furniture—and more.

    In the artful hands at Timber Two, each furniture piece becomes a narrative, an emblem of sustainable living and Australian craftsmanship. Imagine a dining table that's not just a place to eat but a centerpiece of conversation, rich with the history of reclaimed timber. Picture a coffee table that's a mosaic of memories, a fusion of form and function. Our creations extend to entertainment units that are more than mere storage - they're statement pieces, and doors that tell a story at every threshold.

    In the heart of your home, the kitchen, our benchtops stand as a testament to timeless design and functionality. These aren't just surfaces; they're the foundation of family gatherings, a fusion of natural beauty and culinary artistry, tailored to fit the rhythm of your home life

    Envision cabinetry that marries durability with elegance, each piece a testament to the time-honored tradition of woodworking. Our outdoor furniture isn't just designed to weather the elements; it's crafted to bring people together, creating a sustainable and stylish alfresco setting.

    At Timber Two, it's not just about furnishing a room; it's about infusing your space with pieces that speak of nature, heritage, and artistry. These are not just objects; they are conversation starters, sustainably sourced and Australian made, destined to become talking points in your home.

    Ready to transform your space with furniture that has a story as rich as its craftsmanship?

    Chat to one of our designers today and start your journey to a more sustainable, stylish home.
