DISCOVER

Guardians of Australian heritage.

At Timber Two, we're not just furniture makers; we're storytellers and guardians of Australian heritage. Our journey begins with a deep-rooted passion for breathing new life into recycled Aussie hardwood. Nestled in the heart of Mudgee, New South Wales, our workshop is a hub of creativity where history is woven into every unique piece of furniture we craft. We salvage timber with a past, transforming it into pieces that not only tell a story but become a part of your home's narrative.

RECYCLED MATERIALS

NEW LIFE FROM OLD.

At Timber Two, we artfully blend history with modernity, creating custom furniture from recycled building materials. Our sustainability ethos highlights the remarkable journey of recycled timber, inviting clients to discover its equal quality to new hardwood and its unique character. This approach reveals the untold stories behind each piece, fostering an appreciation for the beauty and resilience of reclaimed materials. Our work isn't just about furniture; it's about reimagining and repurposing, giving old timber new life and a new story in your space.
