Timber two’S MISSION

Sustaining Heritage, Crafting Future.

At the core of Timber Two is a mission that intertwines sustainability and heritage with cutting-edge craftsmanship. Our goal transcends the mere creation of furniture; we aim to transform recycled Australian hardwood, rich in history and character, into bespoke pieces that narrate stories. Utilizing state-of-the-art machinery and tools, we blend traditional woodworking techniques with modern technology, ensuring precision, quality, and finesse in every piece. We're not just crafting furniture; we're preserving a legacy, ensuring that the tales and beauty of Australian hardwood are seamlessly woven into the fabric of contemporary living. Each creation is a tribute to Australia's past and a step towards a sustainable future, designed to live on in homes and hearts, bridging generations and fostering a profound appreciation for our history and our environment

Where Skill Meets Art

High-End Craftsmanship.

At Timber Two, high-end craftsmanship is not just a standard; it's our signature. Led by Jake Gilbert, our team combines years of expertise with a passion for perfection. We take pride in our meticulous approach to woodworking, where every cut, joint, and finish is executed with precision. Our commitment to quality is evident in every piece we create, from the smallest detail to the overall design. Our dedication to high-end craftsmanship ensures that every furniture piece is not just durable and functional but also a work of art that stands the test of time.

Blending Tradition with Innovation

An Eye for Design and Industry Trends.

Staying ahead in the world of furniture design means having an eye for both timeless styles and emerging trends. At Timber Two, we strike a balance between traditional techniques and contemporary aesthetics. Our design philosophy revolves around showcasing the natural beauty of recycled timber while adapting to the evolving tastes and needs of our clients. We closely monitor industry trends, ensuring our designs are not just relevant but also trendsetting. By blending innovative design with the rustic charm of Australian hardwood, we create pieces that are both unique and in tune with modern living spaces.
