The Essence of Recycled Timber.

Legacy in the Lumber.

Recycled timber tells a story of Australia's architectural past. Predominantly sourced from the hardwood frames of older homes, it encompasses joists, rafters, studs, and beams, each bearing the marks of history. In bygone days, Australian builders relied on locally sourced hardwood, a practice that not only supported local ecosystems but also ingrained a distinct character in each structure. This timber, hewn from native forests, was chosen for its durability and strength, qualities that remain evident in the recycled wood we use today. By repurposing this timber, we're not just creating furniture; we're preserving a piece of Australian history.

Imperfections Perfected.

The unique appeal of recycled timber lies in its imperfections – the nail holes, checkouts, and other markings that narrate its former life. Each 'defect' is a testament to the timber's journey and adds unparalleled character to every piece. We meticulously fill these marks with resin, ensuring that while their stories are preserved, they don't compromise the finish or integrity of your custom vanity or furniture piece. This careful balance between preserving history and delivering quality craftsmanship is what makes our furniture uniquely charming and durable.
